# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 3, 2026

Hukam shows you the daily Hukamnama and lines of Gurbani. It was built to know as little about you as possible. This policy explains what the app does — and mostly what it doesn't do — with your information.

## The short version

- No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold or shared for marketing.
- You can use everything in Hukam without creating an account.
- Your choices — colours, themes, app icon, notification times — stay on your device.
- We never use anything you do in Hukam to train any AI model.

## What the app sends over the network

Each day the app asks our server which Hukamnama was published. That request carries no account of who you are beyond what any internet request includes (an IP address, handled by our hosting provider's standard infrastructure logs). The scripture itself ships inside the app; the server only names the day's reading.

## If you sign in

Signing in is optional and adds nothing you need for daily use — it exists so future features can keep your saved lines across devices. If you choose **Sign in with Apple**, we store:

- an opaque user identifier issued by Apple, and
- the email address associated with it (which may be Apple's private relay address, if you chose to hide yours).

That is the whole list. It is stored with our authentication provider (Supabase) and used only to operate your account. You can delete your account inside the app — Settings → Account — and deletion removes it from our systems.

## Subscriptions

Hukam Plus is billed by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. What the app itself keeps is a yes-or-no answer to "is Plus active", stored on your device.

## Notifications

Reminders carrying lines of the Hukamnama are scheduled locally on your device. They do not pass through our servers.

## Children

Hukam does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the only personal information it can hold at all is the optional sign-in above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.
